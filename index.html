Designed for Institutional Excellence

Ensuring transparency, student safety, and institutional compliance at every stage.

🎙️

Parent Voice Verification

Parents authorize student leave with recorded voice consent, compared against registered biometric voice profiles to prevent fraudulent submissions.

🔊

Multilingual Voice Reading

Built-in Text-to-Speech synthesis automatically reads out leave letters in Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, or English for parent convenience.

🏢

Smart Hostel Branching

Database-driven automated routing directs Hostellers to Hostel Wardens for gate passes, while Day Scholar requests automatically finalize after HOD sign-off.

⚡

Role-Specific Dashboards

Custom-tailored workspaces for Students, Parents, Class Advisors, Department HODs, and Hostel Wardens with live status badges and audit trails.

📜

Official College Letterheads

Leaves are formatted as formal collegiate letters complete with student register numbers, leave reasons, destinations, and verification stamps.

🔒

Secure & Compliant

Session-based authentication, bcrypt password hashing, and prepared SQL transactions ensure highest data protection standards.
